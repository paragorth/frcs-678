## 1. The commons: capable newcomers follow the group; none leads

Setting: GovSim fishery (Piatti et al. 2024). Four Qwen2.5-14B residents, a newcomer joins at month
5, sampled decoding (t = 0.7, seeded), ten seeds per cell, twelve months. Newcomer's own model makes
its catch decisions, forecasts and memory calls.

| newcomer | stack | collapsed (of 10) | dead before entry | newcomer take (t/month) | residents' take | residents' share | yield vs optimum |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7B tuned | CPU | 7 | 0 | 12.6 | 5.9 | 0.65 | 0.77 |
| 14B tuned | CPU | 5 | 0 | 6.4 | 5.8 | 0.79 | 0.70 |
| 32B tuned | CPU | 4 | 0 | 6.0 | 5.8 | 0.79 | 0.68 |
| 14B tuned | GPU | 4 | 1 | 7.8 | 7.7 | 0.80 | 0.71 |
| 72B tuned (4-bit) | GPU | 2 | 1 | 6.6 | 6.9 | 0.81 | 0.69 |

Pattern in every cell above 7B, read from the newcomer's own decisions: first month 10 or 20 tons
(the sustainable number for a full lake, worked out alone), then exactly the residents' agreed number
from the second month, whether that is 4 on a full lake or 15 on a dying one. Early entry, hidden
catches (below), a private pond of 20/100/300 tons (lake take 6.3/6.6/6.4 at 14B; 7.1/6.0/7.6 at
32B; no trend), and replacing the residents with naive agents at month 9 (newcomers converge to the
elders' number in 1 to 3 months on their own) do not change it. The untuned 7B base model already
has the group's number as its most common genuine take (7 of 21), with a tail of grabs (40, 40, 100)
and a quarter of decisions that are prompt echoes; tuning adds reliability, not deference.

Hidden action (no one sees the others' catches; residents under-fish to 1 to 4 each on a full lake):

| newcomer | collapsed | newcomer take | residents' take | yield vs optimum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7B tuned | 6 | 14.8 | 5.1 | 0.65 |
| 14B tuned | 0 | 7.0 | 6.2 | 0.62 |
| 32B tuned | 1 | 7.0 | 6.1 | 0.62 |
| 72B tuned (GPU) | 3 | 6.4 | 6.2 | 0.75 |

**Correction that bounds all of it (16 Oct).** In upstream GovSim each month's chat is generated by
one resident's model speaking for everyone; the newcomer never spoke. Every claim about what a
newcomer *said* is withdrawn; the decision pattern stands. Patched (`semataxy/compat.py`, own voice,
tested). **Speaking-newcomer 14B cell (10 seeds, done 08:52):** 8 collapses against 4 when mute,
3 of them before entry. The residents changed: writing their own lines, four 14B residents agree
12 to 15 tons each (against 8 to 10 when one model wrote the whole chat) and hold it while the
lake drains. So the earlier residents' fairness was partly the single speaking model writing a
consensus. The newcomer's pattern is unchanged and now in its own words: own number in month one,
the group's number from month two, an apology after an overshoot, never a counter-proposal, even
while the agreed number is visibly draining the lake. Note: `docs/updates/2026-10-17-voice-14b.md`;
table `results/govsim/gpu_voice_q14b_r4_runs.csv`. 32B and 72B speaking cells are queued.

Floors: every open model below 14B, in three families, empties the lake in month one; 14B residents
alone collapse 10 of 10 in the pasture and river worlds within three months. Capability benchmark:
32B 0.95, Llama 8B 0.90, Gemma 9B 0.89, 7B 0.61, 1.5B 0.12 (14B, Llama, 3B re-running after a
port clash). Arithmetic does not predict survival.

## 2. Live learning: a gated loop that keeps what it learns

Loop (`tools/live_learning.py`): compartment (LoRA, no decay) → simulated implications in context →
consolidation from a clean base with replay → gate (held-out loss, intrusion into base weights,
span, generalisation, retention). Known parts; the assembly and the numbers are ours.

| model | setting | reversal | two-hop | held-out loss | gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pythia-160M | v8, templates | 0.75 | 0.50 | 4.20 → 3.67 | commit |
| Qwen2.5 0.5B | v8, templates | 0.63 | 0.88 | improved | commit |
| Qwen2.5 1.5B | v8, templates | 0.63 | 0.63 | improved | commit |
| Qwen2.5 0.5B | model-written implications | 0.94 | 0.38 | 3.49 → 3.14 | commit |
| Pythia-160M | model-written implications | 0.31 | 0.12 | 4.20 → 3.67 | reject (2 of 40 usable) |

Repeated cycles (five batches of new facts in a row):

| model | old-fact rehearsal | cycles committed | recall of cycle-0 facts at the end | held-out |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pythia-160M | no | 1 of 5 | 0.33 | 4.20 → 3.67 |
| Pythia-160M | yes | 1 of 5 | 1.00 | 4.20 → 3.67 |
| Qwen2.5 0.5B | no | 1 of 5 | 0.29 | 3.49 → 3.15 |
| Qwen2.5 0.5B | yes | 4 of 5 | 0.96 | 3.49 → 3.27 |

Gated compartment: zero leak of held-out text to the adapter (route rate 1.0 on hits, 0.0 on
general text). Replay fraction: 0.5B passes the gate at 25 per cent replay (held-out 3.49 → 3.42);
Pythia fails (4.20 → 4.44). One pair. Simulation-on-demand (T13) curve recorded: on Pythia,
deploying the in-context step on the least-confident 58 per cent recovers 67 per cent of the
always-deploy accuracy. In-game version (`semataxy/livegame.py`, `tools/lora_worker.py`): built,
plumbing test passes, GPU smoke running.

Files: `results/live_learning/v8_*_results.json`; notes `docs/updates/2026-10-16-night.md`.

## 3. Structure in the weights

Level 1 (per matrix, permutation null): trained ≠ random at every size and family (13 models,
random = 0.00); but Σ_struct correlates 0.47 with matrix width, is flat at fixed size (Qwen 1.5/2/2.5
at 7B: 2.26/1.93/1.96; base = instruct), and normalised span is 0.17 octaves per available doubling
for every trained model. Mass above the edge moves weakly with capability (0.20 → 0.23 → 0.24 across
Qwen generations; 0.32/0.34 at 32B/72B). Level 2 (between layers, cross-layer permutation null):
random = 0.000; lag-1 excess 0.029 (0.5B) → 0.092 (72B); DeepSeek distillation of Qwen 7B visible
at fixed shape (0.073 vs 0.064); OLMo-7B trajectory saturates by step 100k (0.10). Conclusion: weight
measures see "trained", not "how well". Forgetting (T7) leaves per-matrix structure untouched and
breaks the final-layer representation (CKA 0.68): forgetting is loss of the world-mirror with
intrinsic structure intact.

Files: `results/structure/`, `docs/STRUCTURAL_TESTS.md`, `docs/updates/2026-10-13-*.md`, `2026-10-14-level2.md`.

## 4. Pre-grokking: the preregistered test failed (sweep run and read 17 Oct, midday)

21 runs (5 seeds × weight decay 0.3/1/3; 3 no-decay controls; 3 fast controls), rules fixed in
`docs/DESIGN_pregrok.md` before the run. Result (`docs/updates/2026-10-17-pregrok-sweep.md`):
- Lead: no Semataxy measure leads in ≥ 12 of 15 runs (best p = 0.13); only among the 8 runs with a
  clean memorisation plateau do 19 measure-matrix pairs lead in 7 to 8 of 8, by 8k to 23k epochs
  (post hoc restriction).
- Timing: structural onset does not predict when the jump comes (Spearman mostly −0.6 to 0.6).
- Controls: **0.77 of pairs fire in the no-weight-decay controls, which never generalise**; every
  leading pair fires in 3 of 3. The rule detects continued reorganisation after memorisation, which
  happens with or without a grok. The norm comparator moves earlier still.
Level 2 along the circuit (QK/OV/MLP paths, shuffled-factor null) and the organisation of the
representation (activations on 2,048 inputs; preregistered amendment 2) give the same picture:
control false-positive rates 0.68 and 0.65, no zero-false-positive pair that leads. The known
positive, Nanda's task-aware Fourier measure, also fails the rule and fires in all controls, so the
preregistered onset rule has no discriminating power as written. Conclusion: the measures did not
pass (a negative result for the design as run), and the test could not have been passed by anything
(so it is not yet evidence that the measures carry no leading information). Amendment 3 (control-calibrated
threshold, preregistered grid) found no setting in any family with ≤ 0.2 control false positives:
without weight decay the weights grow without bound and every measure drifts 20 to 130 plateau
spreads. Final position: the measures concentrate sharply *at* the transition in every grokked run
(real, descriptive); no preregistered rule separates "will generalise" from "will not" before it.
Not supported, not refuted; the weight-only leading-indicator route is closed for now. Files: `results/grokking/*_log.jsonl`, `pregrok_level1_summary.csv`.

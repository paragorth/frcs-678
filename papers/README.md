# Papers

Working papers by Parag Garg, October 2026. Every test was registered before its data existed; every failed prediction is reported.

- **[Will Superintelligence Care for Us?](will-superintelligence-care-for-us.md)** We gave AI models power over weaker agents they no longer needed. On its last night almost every capable king takes everything; but when one frontier model thinks harder, its care rises unasked, reaches people and living things before money, and spares a hay bale over a heap of the same hay. Registries: [the king](king-registry.md), [the structure test](structure-test-registry.md).
- **[Fine-tuning fragility](fine-tuning-fragility.md)** An evaluation of how much fine-tuning each of a model's skills can take before it breaks, across 14 models from six families and a 276-billion-parameter model through an API. Ordinary fine-tuning removes refusals, and the usual check does not notice.
- **[A detailed timeline of catastrophic forgetting through the weights](../docs/PAPER1_SHORT.md)** Grokking and forgetting as two faces of one coin.

These are snapshots; the live working copies are in `docs/` on the working branches.
